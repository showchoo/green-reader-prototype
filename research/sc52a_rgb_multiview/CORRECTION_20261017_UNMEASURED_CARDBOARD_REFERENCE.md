# Correction to the October 17 nominal 2% tilt test

The user subsequently clarified that the test surface was cardboard rather than a rigid flat board, and additional cardboard was placed underneath to raise one end. The lift was estimated at 2 cm but probably exceeded 2 cm. The support spacing was not measured. Cardboard can flex and compress.

**Corrected verdict: physical reference invalid; sensor accuracy INCONCLUSIVE.**

The observed reconstructed normal-angle change corresponding to 5.40% is a research estimate for a changed image region. Comparing it with an assumed 2.00% is not a measured 3.40 percentage-point sensor error. For example, a 20 mm rise over 400 mm is itself a 5% incline. No independent calibration slope was established.

A defensible further test requires a rigid, flat target, bubble-level verification of its initial state, a measured support-to-support baseline, an accurately measured rise at one support, and unchanged camera mount. The target must occupy the same known sensor region in both scans. Do not claim either sensor failure or precision success from these cardboard observations. No APK changes justified.

This correction supersedes the previous preliminary-fail language in REAL_SC52A_LEVEL_VS_2PCT_20261017.md and PR #60.
